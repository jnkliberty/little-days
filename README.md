# Little Days

A baby tracker I’m designing for my family.

**Status: in development.** The next build is still under test.

<img src="images/home.png" alt="Little Days home screen with invented feeding, sleep, and diaper records" width="420">

Adults choose their name from a list. The current app supports feeds, sleep, diapers, notes, timeline edits, handoff notes, and care confirmations. It also has English and Spanish controls, unit settings, and a photo gallery.

<img src="images/timeline.png" alt="Timeline with four invented care records and the adults who logged them" width="420">

Child setup still has an unresolved blocker when adults enter conflicting dates. Native-phone testing and restart checks remain unfinished. Reminder settings save preferences only; the app does not send reminders.

Screenshots use invented sample data. The app and family data are private.
